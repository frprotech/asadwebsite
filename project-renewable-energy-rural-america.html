Renewable Energy Works
For Rural America

A comprehensive multimedia campaign produced for the Natural Resources Defense Council (NRDC) and Environmental Entrepreneurs (E2), proving how clean energy investments revitalize farming economies and rural communities.

Project Snapshot

  • Clients / Partners: Natural Resources Defense Council (NRDC) & E2
  • Narrative Producer: Roger Sorkin
  • Key Messengers: Family Farmers, County Commissioners, School Superintendents
  • Strategic Focus: Rural Economic Revitalization, Lease Revenues & Local Tax Bases
  • Core Assets: Field Video Library, Case Study Vignettes, Digital Advocacy Hub
Watch Campaign Clips on E2 →
STRATEGIC CONTEXT

The Strategic Purpose & Advocacy Imperative

Across the American heartland, well-funded special interest groups launched aggressive disinformation campaigns aimed at convincing county commissions to enact moratoriums and zoning bans against wind and solar developments, falsely claiming clean energy harms local communities.

NRDC and Environmental Entrepreneurs (E2) needed an evidence-based, emotionally resonant storytelling initiative to debunk these claims. The mandate was to give voice to the rural landowners, school superintendents, and county commissioners who directly experience the financial benefits of hosting renewable energy infrastructure.

MEASURABLE OUTCOMES

Documented Legislative & Public Impact

Roger Sorkin led field production across agricultural counties, documenting real farmers whose lease payments saved generational family farms from foreclosure, and local school administrators who built modern science labs funded by wind and solar property tax receipts.

  • Defeating Zoning Bans: Testimonials were presented at critical county commission hearings, defeating anti-renewable ordinances in multiple states.
  • Trusted Rural Messengers: Relied strictly on conservative rural community leaders rather than national environmental spokespeople.
  • Persistent Media Library: Created a permanent video and case study repository utilized by clean energy advocates nationwide.

"Clean energy lease income acts as an essential, drought-proof financial shock absorber for rural family farms, allowing growers to keep land in the family for future generations."

Roger Sorkin • Media Campaign Producer
